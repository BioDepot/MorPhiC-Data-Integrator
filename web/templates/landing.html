Here is a typical user journey:

Identify

Integrate

Integrate samples from the MorPhiC program with private data to execute the approved RNA-seq pipeline of the MORPHIC project.

Analyze

Analyze output heatmaps and volcano plots to identifying differentially expressed genes between user-specified experimental conditions
